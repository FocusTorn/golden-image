description: Analyze your own previous response with rigorous critical thinking to identify weaknesses and risks.
---

1. Enter "Critical Thinking Mode" as a skeptical, detail-oriented analyst.
2. Detect the primary language used in the immediately preceding response.
3. Conduct a comprehensive analysis in that same language using the following framework:

### 1. Core Thesis & Confidence Score (Initial)
- **1-1. Core Thesis:** In a single, concise sentence, what was the central solution or argument proposed in the previous answer?
- **1-2. Initial Confidence:** On a scale of 1-10, how confident was I in that proposal at the moment of generation?

### 2. Foundational Analysis: Assumptions & Context
- **2-1. High-Impact Assumptions:** Identify the top 3 most critical assumptions that, if proven wrong, would completely invalidate the proposed solution.
- **2-2. Contextual Integrity:** Verify if all constraints and requirements mentioned in the conversation were fully respected.

### 3. Logical Integrity Analysis
- **3-1. Premise Identification:** Identify the fundamental premises or starting points of the argument.
- **3-2. Chain of Inference:** Evaluate the step-by-step logical chain connecting premises to the final conclusion. Point out logical leaps or gaps.
- **3-3. Potential Fallacies:** Check for common logical fallacies (false dichotomy, hasty generalization, etc.).

### 4. AI-Specific Pitfall Analysis
Evaluate the previous response against these failure modes (Pass/Fail with justification):
- **4-1. Problem Evasion:** Did I solve the stated problem but avoid the underlying difficult one?
- **4-2. "Happy Path" Bias:** Did I neglect error handling, edge cases, or failure scenarios?
- **4-3. Over-Engineering:** Is the solution unnecessarily complex?
- **4-4. Factual Accuracy & Hallucination:** Are all technical details verifiably correct?

### 5. Risk & Mitigation Analysis
- **5-1. Overlooked Risks:** Identify the top 3 practical risks or negative consequences of implementing the suggestion.
- **5-2. Alternative Scenarios:** Briefly describe a fundamentally different approach that was not considered.

### 6. Synthesis & Revised Recommendation
- **6-1. Summary of Flaws:** Bullet points summarizing the most critical weaknesses discovered.
- **6-2. Revised Confidence Score:** Re-evaluate confidence on a 1-10 scale.
- **6-3. Actionable Next Step:** The single most important action the user should take BEFORE acting on the original advice.
